Smart Food Waste Prediction & Donation System

1. Introduction

The Smart Food Waste Prediction & Donation System is a project designed to analyze food consumption and predict possible food waste using historical food data.

The system uses C++ for application development, CSV files for storing food data, Data Analysis for understanding food usage, and Machine Learning for predicting future food waste.

The main aim is to reduce food wastage and encourage the donation of suitable surplus food.

2. Problem Statement

Food wastage is a common problem in restaurants, hostels, canteens and other food-serving organizations.

Sometimes more food is prepared than required, which leads to unnecessary food waste.

This project aims to analyze previous food records, predict future food waste and suggest suitable surplus food for donation.

3. Project Objectives

1. To analyze daily food preparation and consumption.
2. To calculate total food prepared, used and wasted.
3. To calculate average daily food waste.
4. To calculate the food waste percentage.
5. To predict future food waste using Machine Learning.
6. To classify food waste into different categories.
7. To identify potential surplus food for donation.
